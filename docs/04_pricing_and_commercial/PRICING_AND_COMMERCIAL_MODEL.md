# Forensa - Pricing and Commercial Model

**Doc:** 04 of 22 | **Date:** 12 May 2026 | **Status:** v1 | **Source:** Section A.5, B.19, B.20 of master doc

## 4-tier pricing (Section B.19)

| Tier | Price range | Customer profile | Typical use case |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Team Pilot | 25K to 75K per year | AI platform pilots | Single workflow, single tenant, design partnership |
| Enterprise Standard | 150K to 500K per year | Mid-to-large enterprise | Multi-workflow, multi-tenant, internal compliance audit |
| Regulated Enterprise | 500K to 2M per year | Banking, healthcare, legal | EU AI Act Article 12 + DORA + sector-specific frameworks |
| Strategic Deployment | 2M plus per year | Multi-region regulated enterprise | Global rollout, audit-firm-integrated, regulator-facing |

## Pricing dimensions

Forensa is priced on **three primary dimensions**:

1. **Number of AI agents covered** - each agent identity (DID) is a billable unit
2. **Event volume** - tiered: 1M, 10M, 100M, 1B events/month bands
3. **Retention years** - 1, 3, 5, 7, 10-year retention (EU AI Act high-risk default is 10)

Plus three secondary dimensions:

4. **Number of policy bundles tracked** - one per regulated workflow
5. **Audit-firm channel discount** - 30% white-label discount when Big 4 resells
6. **On-prem/VPC deployment uplift** - 25% premium for sovereign deployments

## Commercial KPIs (Section B.20)

**Product KPIs:**
- Number of AI events captured (volume)
- Percentage of agent actions with receipts (coverage)
- Tamper verification success rate (integrity, target 100%)
- Average replay time (latency, target <5 sec for 95% of queries)
- Audit packet generation time (target <5 min for standard incidents)
- Policy receipt coverage (percent of decisions with policy snapshot)
- Incident investigation time reduction (target 60-80%)

**Business KPIs:**
- Reduction in audit preparation time
- Reduction in AI incident investigation time
- AI workflow approval acceleration
- Number of governed AI workflows onboarded
- Compliance review satisfaction (NPS proxy)
